📄 System Architecture & Whitepaper

1. Programmatic Escrow Lifecycle

Phase 1: Job & Budgeting

Buyer posts project budget in USD. The server queries Gemini's live public ticker (`api.gemini.com/v1/pubticker/solusd`) to compute exact lamports and locks the pricing snapshot.

Phase 2: Vault Initialization

Each job generates a dedicated on-chain Solana escrow account. The platform wallet sponsors the rent-exempt minimum so the vault is instantly addressable.

Phase 3: Client-Side Funding

Buyer clicks "Fund Escrow". The in-browser wallet generates and signs an Ed25519 `SystemProgram.transfer` transaction client-side and broadcasts to Devnet.

Phase 4: Work & Settlement

Freelancer delivers milestone. Upon buyer approval (or dispute arbitration), the vault releases funds directly to the freelancer's wallet, recorded on Solscan.
