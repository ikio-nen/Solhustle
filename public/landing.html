ESCROW LIFECYCLE BLUEPRINT

PHASES 01 — 04
01

Job Scope & USD/SOL Snapshot

Buyer posts requirements denominated in USD. Gemini public ticker locks the pricing snapshot and derives exact on-chain lamports.

02

Escrow Vault Initialization

A unique Solana keypair is generated for the job. Platform wallet sponsors rent exemption, establishing a stable devnet vault address.

03

Client-Side Ed25519 Funding

Buyer confirms escrow funding. Browser modal builds a SystemProgram.transfer, signs with Ed25519 keypair, and broadcasts to Devnet.

04

Delivery Approval & Release

Freelancer submits milestone deliverable. Upon buyer approval, escrow releases locked SOL directly to the freelancer's wallet.
