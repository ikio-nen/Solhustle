Inside the product

How it works

01 · Post the work

Describe the job and set a budget in USD. It is priced against the live SOL ticker and that snapshot is frozen for the contract.

02 · Lock the budget

One click signs a transfer into a vault that belongs to this job alone. The platform covers the rent so the address exists straight away.

03 · Deliver the work

Your freelancer pitches, delivers with a proof link, and the contract moves to a delivered state that only you can settle.

04 · Release or dispute

Approving pays straight out of the vault. If you disagree, the same vault goes to arbitration instead of a payment.

Why escrow

The money can't move on its own

Funds sit in a vault neither side controls alone. Nothing is released without an approval or an arbiter's ruling, so a bad week for either party can't turn into a lost payment.

Every settlement is verifiable

Each deposit, release and refund is a real devnet transaction with a signature you can check on Solscan. There is nothing to take on faith and no ledger to argue about.

Disagreements have an exit

Escalate a stuck contract and it lands in an arbitration queue with both sides' evidence attached. The ruling releases the vault one way or the other.

FAQ

Do I need a crypto wallet extension?

No. Keys are generated for your account on the server and used to sign inside the page, so the flow works in any browser without installing anything.

What does it cost?

Only the Solana network fee, roughly 0.000005 SOL per transaction. The vault's rent deposit is returned when the contract closes.

What happens if the freelancer disappears?

The contract can be escalated to arbitration, and a ruling can refund the vault to you. The funds never sit in limbo with no way out.

Is this running on mainnet?

Not yet — everything settles on Solana devnet while the escrow program is still an account-based MVP rather than a deployed Anchor program.
